# Stay22 Creator Commerce

**Retail Innovation Challenge 2026 · Team RICE · McGill University (Desautels) · Semi-finalist**

[Read the portfolio case study](https://chuyu-xu.github.io/projects/stay22-creator-commerce/)

## Project overview

Our team explored how Stay22 could create additional commercial value from its existing travel-content audience. We proposed Creator Commerce: a service connecting independent hotels with relevant, opted-in travel creators through managed, disclosed campaigns and booking performance measurement.

The proposed journey runs from a hotel's campaign brief through creator matching, sponsored content, booking handoff, and validated-stay reporting. The commercial model combines a campaign service fee with contracted performance fees. Campaign operations and booking verification would require development or integration.

## From idea to implementation strategy

Phase 1 explained the service and customer experience. Phase 2 developed the proposed workstreams, resources, readiness milestones, and commercial review gates. The recommendation was to run a controlled pilot and make further investment conditional on validated evidence.

The team's proposed evaluation distinguished attributed bookings from incremental value, considered displaced affiliate earnings, and separated early engagement signals from validated outcomes.

## Competition outcome and proposed value

**Our team advanced to the semi-finals.** We delivered Phase 1 and Phase 2 presentations containing the business concept and implementation strategy.

The intended benefits were coordinated creator campaigns for hotels, additional paid collaboration opportunities for creators, relevant labelled offers for travellers, and complementary service revenue for Stay22. These remained hypotheses to validate. No launched pilot, signed customers, adoption by Stay22, or realized financial impact is claimed.
