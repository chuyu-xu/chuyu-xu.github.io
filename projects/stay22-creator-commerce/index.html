01Business problem

A new service built on existing travel intent

Stay22 helps travel publishers and creators monetize booking intent through affiliate relationships. Our team investigated a complementary opportunity: helping independent hotels coordinate creator campaigns and understand their booking outcomes.

The question was whether Stay22’s existing relationships and travel-content expertise could support a commercially viable service. Team research, competitive assessment, and illustrative financial scenarios informed the proposal.

02Proposed solution

Creator Commerce: brief, match, measure

Hotels would fund managed campaigns with relevant, opted-in travel creators. The proposed model combined a campaign service fee with contracted performance fees on eligible, validated bookings.

  1. 01Hotel brief

    Audience, budget, room offer

  2. 02Creator match

    Relevant content and opt-in

  3. 03Sponsored content

    Approved, labelled placements

  4. 04Booking handoff

    Hotel or authorized provider

  5. 05Validate & report

    Eligible stays and payouts

Intended value

Hotels: coordinated campaigns. Creators: paid collaborations. Travellers: relevant, labelled offers. Stay22: complementary service revenue.

A proposed service, with campaign operations and booking verification to develop or integrate. Customer demand and business benefits remained hypotheses to validate.

03Phase 2 · Implementation

A staged pilot with clear dependencies

The plan coordinated five workstreams: hotel partnerships, creator network, product and technology, legal and compliance, and measurement and finance.

Required support: pilot lead, hotel sales, creator coordination, engineering, data and legal input, plus hotel and booking-integration partners.

04Risk & recommendation

Readiness first. Commercial evidence next.

RiskProposed response
Hotel demand & creator participationLimited paid pilot, renewal assessment, creator opt-in, and clear compensation.
Traveller trust & partner termsDisclose sponsorship before the click, avoid best-price claims, and review contractual terms.
Attribution ≠ incrementalityCompare with the current OTA offer and account for displaced affiliate earnings.
Integration delaysConfirm the partner, test verification, and pause launch if unresolved.
